# Tips open at the pointer

Cited as D-L80.

A tip, the words that open when the pointer rests on a control, opens at the pointer: below and to the right of where it rested, clear of the arrow, as the system's own tips sit, and above it when the window's bottom is too near. It stays where it opened while the pointer stays on the control, even as a render redraws the control under it. Until now a tip was placed over the control's middle, so where it opened depended on the control's size: a button's tip sat just above the button, a settings row's sat over the row's centre, half a screen from a pointer resting at the row's end, and a wide card's further still. Three controls used the browser's `title` attribute instead, each opening in the browser's own time, face and place, and silent to a screen reader on a keyboard-focused control. Every tip is now a `data-tip`, shown by one module (`webview/ui/hover.mjs`) in one face at one delay, and no interface module writes a `title` attribute or a tip of its own (`tests/scripts/conventions.test.js`, `browser-tests/tooltips.spec.js`). A control reached by the keyboard has no pointer on it, so its tip opens over the control's middle as before. The hover card is not a tip: it opens beside its key, where the pointer is anyway, and its side cards beside it. The contract is in [Shared controls](../spec/interface-contracts.md#shared-controls) and [Accessibility](../spec/interface-contracts.md#accessibility).

## Considered options

Keeping the tip at the control, the rule this replaces: it reads as one rule in the code and as a different one on every screen, since the eye measures the tip from the pointer, not from the control's bounds it cannot see. The browser's `title` attribute everywhere: one line per control and no placement code, but its delay, face and position are the browser's, it never shows for keyboard focus, and it cannot carry the read-only reasons and shortcut words Ark's tips do. Following the pointer as it moves: the tip would shake under a hand reading a row, and cover what the pointer is pointing at.
