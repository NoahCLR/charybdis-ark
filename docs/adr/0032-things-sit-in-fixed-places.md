# Things sit in fixed places

Cited as D-L32.

Repeated elements reserve fixed positions, the interface keeps what a layer stores distinct from what a composed layer view reaches, and shared reach sections keep their identity across the workbench tabs. Stable positions let people compare rows and switch editors without losing their place, and keeping storage and activation apart stops a preview from misreporting what an edit changes. The rules are in [Interface rendering and rule ownership](../architecture/interface-contracts.md#fixed-positions-and-composed-reach).
