# A long hold comes after the hold

Cited as D-L82.

A behaviour's Long hold threshold is later than its Tap / hold threshold, on every behaviour, and Ark's editors refuse an edit that would put it at or before. Each threshold is the behaviour's own or, left empty, the default it follows: the behaviour or dual-role tap / hold threshold and the long hold threshold under Tap & hold timing. The keyboard accepts the reverse and orders the two thresholds itself, but then no press is a Hold before it is a Long hold: with both tiers tapping on release the Hold can never run, and with other hold styles which tier fires depends on hold style and scan order (firmware `test_release_timing_intervals`). Review only warned about it (D-L49), after the edit was made, though nobody sets thresholds that way on purpose. A behaviour with no Long hold is held to it too, so the rule reads the same on every behaviour and adding a Long hold later never meets timings already out of order.

An edit is refused, in words naming each behaviour, when it saves a behaviour out of order, makes one (a new behaviour, or one moved onto a dual-role key), or moves the timings of one: a change to the defaults, the two behaviour defaults against each other included, or a custom key's shortcut becoming a tap-toggle or one-shot one, which makes its behaviour wait the dual-role threshold. A profile that holds timings out of order, read from the keyboard or imported, opens as it is and takes other edits. Review keeps warning about it, and saving one of its behaviours needs the order fixed first. The behaviour editor also marks it as the person types: a timing that breaks the order stays in its field, edged red, with the reason under the fields, until a timing mends it or the panel closes, while core's refusal is said in the rail as any refused edit's is. One rule and one sentence decide it for core and the editor (`rules/hold-order.mjs`; core's `holdOrderRefusal`, `core/model/gesture-timing.js`; `tests/model/gesture-timing.test.js`, `tests/model/key-behavior-edits.test.js`, `tests/model/settings-editor.test.js`, `tests/model/custom-key-editor.test.js`). How the checks read timings is in [Timing, built-in branches and reachability](../architecture/timing-and-reachability.md).

## Considered options

Holding only behaviours with a Long hold: the long hold threshold does nothing without one, but adding a Long hold would then be refused for timings the person set earlier, and the rule would read differently from behaviour to behaviour. Leaving it to Review's warning, as before: the editor accepted timings no one means, and the warning came after the fact. Refusing a profile that already holds them: the keyboard accepts it, so a profile read from it must open, and refusing every edit until it is fixed would block unrelated work. Holding the multi tap window to an order too: it is timed from releases, not from the press, so no order between it and the hold thresholds makes a tier unreachable.
