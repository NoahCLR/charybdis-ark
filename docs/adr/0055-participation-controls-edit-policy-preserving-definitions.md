# Participation controls edit policy and keep definitions

Cited as D-L55.

Ark exposes the firmware's participation contract at every scope it has — the master and source-layer switches in Settings, enable and allowed-layer controls on each shared behaviour and combo, **Use behaviour** and **Join combos** on a physical placement, and the two combo-output permissions — as ordinary draft edits through Review, Undo and Discard. Switching something off changes only where it runs and keeps its definition and timing, so a person can bypass a behaviour on one key (a gaming key, say) without losing it; a bypassed native key falls through, and a bypassed custom key sends its shortcut or nothing (D-L63). Firmware owns the rules themselves in [participation-policy.md](../../upstream/firmware/docs/spec/participation-policy.md); Ark's combo-output contract is in [interface-contracts.md](../spec/interface-contracts.md#combo-output-behaviour-permissions).
